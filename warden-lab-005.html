Lab rules, risk assumptions, and fill model
  • Practice account: $10,000. Modeled risk budget: $100 per trade. MES: $5 per point; fictional round-trip fee $1.50 per contract. Planning allowance: 0.50 points per contract.
  • One trade maximum per attempt. Flat students may buy, sell short, or wait at each of the three checkpoints. Once that trade closes, continue observing; no re-entry or adding in this version.
  • Every entry uses a displayed checkpoint bracket. You may hold that bracket or exit early at the next decision. Stops cannot be widened or removed. An entry decision is not a claim that a strategy has a proven edge.
  • Checkpoint planning quote: bid is last close minus 0.25; ask is last close plus 0.25. Actual market fills are supplied at 0.50 points worse than the last close: higher for buys, lower for sells. The extra 0.25 relative to the quote is modeled execution slippage.
  • Stops are modeled stop-market exits filled 0.25 points beyond the stop, adversely. Targets receive explicit full fills at their price. The paired exit is canceled after a full exit. Real touch-to-fill and OCO behavior are not guaranteed.
  • For this synthetic exercise only, each rising bar follows open → low → high → close; each falling bar follows open → high → low → close. This scripted path resolves exit ordering. Real OHLC bars alone do not reveal intrabar event order.
  • Trades still open at the exercise end are closed at a supplied market fill 0.50 points adverse to the final close. Slippage is included in actual fills, not deducted again as a separate planning cost.
  • All volume is classified in this example: volume = ask-executed + bid-executed contracts; delta = ask-executed − bid-executed contracts. No number identifies the traders or guarantees the next move.
  • Planning budgets and protective orders do not guarantee maximum losses. Timestamps and journals are browser-generated practice records, not tamper-proof certification records.
