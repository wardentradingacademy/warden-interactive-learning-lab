Checkpoint 1 of 3 — Plan the move

The opening position

The fictional ES market has advanced from 4,984 toward resistance at 5,004. The latest completed five-minute candle closed at 5,000.00. Buyers have made upward progress, but continuation is unconfirmed.

Simulation account: $10,000. Approved trade budget: $100, including the stated cost and slippage allowance. Margin is not the risk budget.

Read the execution assumptions and lab rules
  • One long entry opportunity only. Choose an order or remain flat. No short entry, adding, re-entry, or partial fills in this exercise.
  • The fixed protective stop is 4,992.00; the target is 5,016.00. The exercise plan prohibits widening or removing the stop. An early full exit is permitted with a reason.
  • MES: $5 per point, $1.50 round-trip fees per contract. ES: $50 per point, $6 round-trip fees per contract. These are fictional fee assumptions.
  • For planning, add 0.50 points of slippage allowance per contract. This allowance is a budgeting estimate, not a maximum-loss guarantee.
  • Hypothetical quote: bid 5,000.00 / ask 5,000.25. Prices are scenario assumptions, not a depth-of-market model. Fills are explicitly supplied at each reveal; a real limit touch would not guarantee a fill.
  • Stops are modeled as stop-market exits. Completed exits cancel the paired target in this simplified exercise. Real order handling must be verified with the platform and broker.
