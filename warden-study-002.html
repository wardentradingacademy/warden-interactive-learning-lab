Instructor debrief — The decision process

In this fictional sequence, the initial breakout above 6020 fails, then price retests 6020 from below and moves lower. That outcome does not prove that an immediate short was a good decision. A trader needed a defensible thesis, clear invalidation, and risk controls before acting.

The Grandmaster Principle: A good decision can lose. A bad decision can win. We evaluate what the trader knew, what they planned, and how they responded—not just where price eventually went.
