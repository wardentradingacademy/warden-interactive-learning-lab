Model assumptions and formulas

Remaining equity = starting equity × (1 − risk fraction)number of losses.

Drawdown = (starting equity − remaining equity) ÷ starting equity.

Required recovery gain = (starting equity ÷ remaining equity − 1) × 100%.

The illustration assumes exact fractional sizing, a fixed percentage of remaining equity, full planned losses, and no deposits, withdrawals, fees, or slippage. Actual contract quantities require rounding, and actual losses can differ from planned losses.

Before the next trade

Try a $50,000 account with five losses at 1%, then at 10%. Compare the remaining equity and required recovery. Next, lower risk and examine how much capital another five losses would leave under those assumptions.

A recovery percentage measures the arithmetic needed to return to the starting balance. Use your trading plan to decide whether the next opportunity qualifies, what risk is currently permitted, and when to reduce exposure or stop for review.
