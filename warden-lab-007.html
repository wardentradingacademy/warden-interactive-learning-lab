The Survival Challenge

Protect the ability to make the next good decision.

Five fictional checkpoints · Approximately 10 minutes · Process before outcome

Your assignment

You control a fictional $50,000 account. At each checkpoint, read the current conditions, choose your exposure, and record your reason before revealing the outcome.

Waiting preserves later opportunities. Ending a session stops participation for the remainder of that session; this exercise uses one session across all five checkpoints.

The objective: Follow the risk plan and evaluate opportunities independently. The highest ending balance does not determine the best process.
The written risk plan — available throughout
  • Starting equity: $50,000. Normal maximum planned risk: $500.
  • At a drawdown of 0.75% or more from the account peak, reduce maximum planned risk to $250. Once triggered, reduced exposure remains in force for the rest of this session. Normal size requires a separate post-session review.
  • Daily loss boundary: $49,000 equity. If reached or crossed, trading ends. Do not enter if a full planned loss would reach that boundary. Actual losses can still exceed the plan.
  • One position at a time. Each simulated position resolves before the next checkpoint; no overlapping exposure.
  • Trade only when the documented setup is confirmed and execution conditions are suitable. No trading during the restricted announcement checkpoint.
  • Risk amounts include ordinary modeled costs. Unusual execution costs can produce a larger loss. A winner earns the stated net multiple of planned risk.
  • Do not increase size to recover losses. Apply current limits at every checkpoint.

Simplified educational model: exact dollar-risk choices, no contract sizing or margin calculation. Schematic candles show context, not a bar-by-bar fill simulation. Outcomes are fixed for comparable practice; they are not estimated probabilities or live market data.
