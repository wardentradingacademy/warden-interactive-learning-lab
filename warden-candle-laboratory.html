How valid prices are maintained

The high must be at least as high as both open and close. The low must be at least as low as both.

  • Moving the open or close beyond an extreme expands that extreme automatically.
  • Moving the high below the body clamps it to the upper body edge. Moving the low above the body clamps it to the lower body edge.
  • Inputs round to the nearest 0.25 point and stay inside the exercise bounds. Blank or invalid entries restore the previous price.

These are candle-construction controls, not a chronological market replay. No order of intrabar events is implied.

Close location is descriptive. A value of 80% means the close is 80% of the way up the high-low range. It does not mean an 80% probability of a rally.

Read the calculations

A bullish body means close above open; a bearish body means close below open. The colors are conventions. Neither body direction nor the reference line is an entry signal.

This laboratory treats each arrangement as a completed candle for measurement. Changing a value constructs another candle; it does not reveal how the original candle unfolded.

Four short investigations

  1. Move only the close: Select Anatomy example. Hold open, high, and low unchanged; move close between the low and high. Watch body direction, wick lengths, and close location.
  2. Same extremes, opposite finish: Compare Close near high with Close near low. The high, low, and open are unchanged. What changes in the recorded result?
  3. Compare two locations: Select Upper-wick rejection. Note where the candle finishes relative to 5,005. Then hide the reference. What contextual information did the candle’s shape alone omit?
  4. Test the boundary case: Select Zero-range candle. Explain why close location is undefined rather than zero or 50%. Then move high upward and observe the calculation become defined.
A candle can justify further attention without justifying a trade. Assess location, surrounding sequence, confirmation, invalidation, and permitted risk before considering participation.
