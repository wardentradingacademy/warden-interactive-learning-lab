Prepare before the replay

Three shuffled fictional ES cases contain four decision checkpoints each: twelve locked decisions. At each checkpoint you see completed five-minute candles, total volume, the calculated session VWAP, and structural references known before the replay.

The session is a fictional teaching template beginning at 08:30 Central. Before the first displayed candle, the included earlier bar history contains 5,000 contracts with a volume-weighted representative price of 5,240.00. Each new completed bar updates that seed using (high + low + close) ÷ 3, weighted by its volume. This is a bar-based VWAP approximation, not transaction-level execution data.

Blind replay: future bars and case outcomes stay hidden in the interface until you lock the required decision. Chart scales use only known references and visible data. This is browser-based practice, not a secure or proctored assessment; do not inspect source data for future bars.

Classify the visible evidence, choose an action, and explain both your reasoning and what would change it. A conditional long or short means the visible setup already supports considering that candidate, subject to your stated conditions. It is not a trade placed by this lab.

Written reasoning is recorded for review, not automatically graded. Selection alignment is scored separately using the evidence available at the checkpoint; later profit or loss is not scored.

Conditional candidate selections receive credit only where supported at that checkpoint. Text length is not an assessment of reasoning quality, and this practice is not certification.

Review your process

  1. Did you define the session VWAP and separate location from directional control?
  2. Did you name the structure and the evidence that supported your classification?
  3. Did your action follow your explanation, including reasons to wait or stand aside?
  4. For each candidate, did you give invalidation, an objective and a risk condition?
  5. Did you distinguish information known at the checkpoint from the later outcome?
