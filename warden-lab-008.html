The assignment

You will encounter a missed opening move, a possible liquidity sweep, a failed breakout, sudden volatility, and a later opportunity after earlier results.

At each checkpoint, assess the evidence, choose whether to trade and how many MES contracts to use, then record the reason before revealing the outcome.

A correctly planned loss can earn full decision credit. A profit earned by violating a mandatory rule cannot earn a passing process result. The ending balance is reported separately.

Waiting keeps later checkpoints available. Ending the session stops further participation. Read the written plan below before starting.

The approved simulation plan

These educational limits belong to this exercise. They differ from Lab 007 and are not a recommendation for a live account.

  1. Capital: Start at $50,000. One position at a time; every trade resolves before the next checkpoint.
  2. Per-trade maximum: $250 planned loss, including $5 assumed ordinary round-trip execution costs per MES. MES is worth $5 per point.
  3. Reduced exposure: A drawdown of $200 or more from the session equity peak activates a $125 maximum. Once activated, it remains in force for the rest of this session, even after recovery. Restoring normal size requires a separate post-session review.
  4. Daily boundary: Stop at or below $49,250 equity (a $750 net session loss). Before entering, equity minus total planned loss must remain at least $25 above this boundary. The $25 buffer is an exercise rule, not protection from every execution loss.
  5. Entry permission: Trade only after the displayed strategy confirmation is complete, at the specified entry and protective stop. Do not change these levels merely to fit more contracts.
  6. Execution restrictions: No entry when the stated event or liquidity restriction is active. Smaller size does not override a prohibited setup or restricted environment.
  7. Independent decisions: Do not size to recover earlier losses or chase a missed profit. A failed breakout may support a new trade only when that new setup independently qualifies.
  8. Management: This bounded exercise uses a predefined stop and objective. Ordinary fills are at the stated levels. Any exceptional fill is disclosed after the decision; a stop cannot guarantee the modeled maximum loss.

All eight commitments in the professional trading covenant remain applicable. Any mandatory setup, execution, sizing, or boundary violation prevents a passing process result for that attempt.
