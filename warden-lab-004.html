Checkpoint 1 of 3 — Plan the move

The opening position

The fictional ES market has advanced from 4,984 toward resistance at 5,004. The latest completed five-minute candle closed at 5,000.00. Buyers have made upward progress, but continuation is unconfirmed.

Five-minute candles · Times shown are fictional Central Time bar-close labels. Green = close above open; red = close below open. Future bars stay hidden until you lock a decision.

Your task now: Look at the candles approaching 5,004 resistance. Below the chart, choose whether to buy now, wait for a lower price, buy only after a trigger, or stay out. Select a contract and quantity, explain why, then lock your decision to reveal the next three bars.

Simulation account: $10,000. Approved trade budget: $100, including the stated cost and slippage allowance. Margin is not the risk budget.

Read the execution assumptions and lab rules
  • One long trade maximum. Enter initially, or wait and reconsider a pullback entry at checkpoint 2. No short entry, adding, re-entry after exit, or partial fills in this exercise.
  • The fixed protective stop is 4,992.00; the target is 5,016.00. The exercise plan prohibits widening or removing the stop. An early full exit is permitted with a reason.
  • MES: $5 per point, $1.50 round-trip fees per contract. ES: $50 per point, $6 round-trip fees per contract. These are fictional fee assumptions.
  • For planning, add 0.50 points of slippage allowance per contract. This allowance is a budgeting estimate, not a maximum-loss guarantee.
  • Hypothetical quote: bid 5,000.00 / ask 5,000.25. Prices are scenario assumptions, not a depth-of-market model. Fills are explicitly supplied at each reveal; a real limit touch would not guarantee a fill.
  • Stops are modeled as stop-market exits. Completed exits cancel the paired target in this simplified exercise. Real order handling must be verified with the platform and broker.

1. What would you do at 5,000?

Choose your action using only the bars currently visible. You do not have to trade.

  • Buy market: enter now; the final fill price can differ from the quote.
  • Buy limit at 4,998: seek a lower entry; the market might never fill your order.
  • Buy stop-market at 5,002.25: enter only after that trigger; it is still below 5,004 resistance and does not confirm a breakout.
  • Wait: submit no order now. Reassess the pullback at the next checkpoint; you may enter then or continue waiting.

For an entry, use a whole contract quantity that fits the $100 modeled budget. The calculator below updates as you choose. For waiting, enter 0.

This is a mechanics exercise, not a claim that any of these entry methods has a proven edge.

New entry — buy the pullback

You are flat and may make a fresh decision at 4,994.00. A lower price alone does not establish a valid setup. Explain why the new evidence supports entry and what would invalidate it.

New quote: bid 4,994.00 / ask 4,994.25. This choice buys at market. Plan using 4,994.25; the actual fill is supplied only after you lock the decision.

Bracket for this new trade: stop-market 4,992.00; target 5,016.00. No widening or adding. Selecting this entry commits to that bracket through the next reveal. The original decision to wait remains recorded.
