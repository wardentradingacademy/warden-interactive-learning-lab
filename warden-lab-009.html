Your assignment

A five-minute ES candle is approaching the previous day’s high. You will see it developing, then completed, then the next completed candle. Before the first reveal, write both a bullish and a bearish possibility.

This is a decision audit, not an order simulator. Trade selections are proposed orders at that checkpoint; they do not create positions or fills. Each decision is locked before new evidence appears. Waiting preserves the next opportunity.

The exercise’s written plan

  • A qualified trade requires a completed breakout or rejection candle, followed by a completed retest that supports the thesis. Intrabar entries are outside this exercise’s plan.
  • Use the supplied checkpoint entry price for any proposed trade. Choose your own structural stop, objective, and whole MES contract count.
  • Maximum estimated risk: $250 per proposed trade. MES is $5 per point per contract. Include a fictional $5 per-contract allowance for round-trip costs and slippage. This allowance cannot guarantee a loss limit.
  • Minimum planned reward/risk: 1:1 after the same allowance. A stop or target must be at least one tick (0.25 point) from entry, on the correct side.
  • Any trade that breaks the setup or risk rules prevents a passing automated process check. Choosing to stand aside is valid; it records no trade proposal.
